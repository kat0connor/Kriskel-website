About Riley

Required Invitation — known around the barn as "Riley" — is a 2007 AQHA/APHA double-registered stallion, the only double-registered son of the legendary Invitation Only. He is out of Sonny Hawkette, an AQHA Leading Dam with $76,000+ LTE and the dam of a Congress Champion.
