Incentive Programs

Currently Nominated

Programs

Super Sires

Super Sires is a stallion incentive program promoting quality bloodlines and supporting breeders, owners, and riders through competitive events.

Breeders' Trust

Breeders' Trust awards cash dividends for points earned by participating horses at APHA-approved events.

Cash for Color

Ca$h for Color is a stallion service auction and incentive program that promotes APHA-registered and color-breed stallions, allowing nominated offspring to compete for cash prizes.
